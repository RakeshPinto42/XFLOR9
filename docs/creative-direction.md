# XPLOR9 creative direction

Agreed 2026-11-23. This is the standard every environment, character, sound and screen is judged against.

## Vision

XPLOR9 is an interactive, cinematic, animated universe of Indian childhood between 1990 and 2010, not a
conventional website with a retro theme. It combines cinematic 3D animation, authentic Indian places
and memories, immersive sound, interactive objects and characters, smooth camera work, and nostalgic
interaction design.

Benchmark: emotionally engaging animated-film storytelling combined with small open-world exploration.
We never copy any studio's distinctive characters, designs or copyrighted material.

## The world

- Indian neighbourhoods and traditional houses; monsoon streets, dark green trees, mist and rain.
- Schools, playgrounds, neighbourhood shops, cassette shops, CRT televisions and old radios.
- Grandparents' homes, libraries and other childhood spaces.
- Original animated characters doing everyday things.

**First major environment: a dark, misty Indian monsoon neighbourhood.** The F1 living room is the
quality benchmark for interiors and will become a home inside this neighbourhood.

**Avoid:** generic cyberpunk, excessive neon, cheap low-poly environments, static 3D scenes, brand names
and logos (no real TV channels, manufacturers or products), imitations of existing characters.

## The five modules live inside the world

| Module | Where it lives (direction, refined per phase) |
|---|---|
| Radio | A radio corner at home and a neighbourhood shop with old radios |
| Television | The family CRT television in the living room |
| Music | A cassette shop on the neighbourhood street |
| Games | Playground, school and a gaming corner |
| Reading Room | A library and a grandparent's home |

The functional interfaces (station lists, players, the book reader) always remain usable, accessible
page content. The 3D world surrounds them; it never replaces them.

## Creative acceptance criteria (every environment)

An environment is accepted only when all of these hold:

1. **Atmosphere.** Deliberate lighting (time of day, warm and cool sources), weather or air (rain, mist,
   dust, wind) and visible life; nothing is a static diorama.
2. **Authenticity.** Recognisably Indian and of 1990–2010 through specific, original details; no brands.
3. **Composition.** A designed establishing shot and at least one cinematic camera move or transition.
4. **Motion.** Environmental animation (rain, fog drift, foliage, fans, flicker) and, where characters
   exist, natural movement.
5. **Interaction.** Meaningful interactive objects that lead into module features.
6. **Sound.** Ambience for the place plus interaction sounds, using the shared audio system.
7. **Quality modes.** Full, Lite and Still all work; reduced motion and no-WebGL are handled.
8. **Performance.** Meets the desktop budget measured on the reference laptop (see roadmap). No
   mobile claims until measured on real devices.
9. **Accessibility.** All functions reachable without the 3D view; text is real text.
10. **Rights.** Every model, texture, sound and piece of music has a recorded source and licence.

## Audio direction

- Environmental ambience (rain, thunder, birds, neighbourhood life), original retro interaction sounds,
  radio and television audio, original nostalgic music and jingles, character dialogue, spatial sound
  where it helps.
- Separate volumes for ambience, music and sound effects, plus a master mute; respects browser autoplay
  rules (nothing plays before the visitor interacts) and remembered preferences.
- Only original or properly licensed audio. Copyrighted music and TV recordings are never assumed to
  be available.

## Honest constraint

Animated-film quality depends on art: characters, environments, textures, animation and sound made by
skilled artists. Code, lighting and CC0 assets can establish the technical foundation and a strong
benchmark, but the final look needs commissioned or carefully produced original assets in content
phases. Each milestone will say plainly how close it is to the bar.
